Questions

Quick answers before you order.

How do I order?

Message us on WhatsApp with your photo and the size you want. We'll confirm the price and what happens next.

Which photo should I send?

Send the clearest, original photo you have, not a screenshot or a photo forwarded through social media. If it needs improving, we'll tell you.

Canvas or glass frame?

Canvas has a soft, classic look and costs less. A glass frame gives a sleek, polished finish.

Can I get a custom size?

Yes. If your size isn't on our list, just message us and ask.

Can I order as a gift?

Yes. Tell us it's a gift and we'll help you choose a size and style.

How long will it take?

It depends on the size and how busy we are. Message us and we'll give you a time for your order.

Ready to order?

Send us your photo and the size you want on WhatsApp. We'll reply with the price and next steps.
